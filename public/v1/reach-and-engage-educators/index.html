Reach matters. Relevance matters more.

Sending the wrong message to more schools doesn’t make it more right.

Identify the educators who matter and create reasons for them to engage with you.

EdCo helps organisations identify the educators who matter and create reasons for them to engage.

This may include

  • Targeted campaigns
  • Event recruitment
  • Content distribution
  • Educator networks
  • Opted-in audiences
  • Research participation
  • CPD
  • Resources
  • Account-based engagement

Teachers use social media. That doesn’t mean they’re there to hear from your brand.
