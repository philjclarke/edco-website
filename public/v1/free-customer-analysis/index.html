Most organisations know who their customers are. Rather fewer know what they have in common, which of them are at risk, or where the next twenty sit.

  • Customers under significant financial pressure
  • Trusts where you already serve some schools but not others
  • Customers sitting inside trusts where you have no central relationship
  • Changes in leadership or structure
  • Organisations similar to your strongest customers
