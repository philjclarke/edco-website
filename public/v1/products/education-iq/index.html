Start with the school, not the product.

The right context, at the point it matters.

Education IQ helps organisations understand individual schools, trusts and the wider education sector so they can identify need, make every interaction more relevant and build better relationships with educators.

Ten ways to use it

Use Education IQ directly, connect it to your systems, or build new solutions and processes with it.

  1. Use as a standalone reference platform

    Search, explore and understand any school, trust or education group to:

    • Understand context and changes
    • Quickly find the facts you need

  2. CRM click-thru for instant context

    Click a button in your CRM to open the Education IQ school or trust dashboard to:

    • Prepare for calls and meetings
    • Produce winning proposals

  3. API enrichment for your CRM data

    Use the Education IQ API to match, cleanse, validate and enrich your existing CRM records to:

    • Improve data quality
    • Reduce maintenance costs

  4. Build your own apps with our data and APIs

    Use Education IQ data and APIs as the intelligence behind your own apps, dashboards and AI tools.

    • Power internal and external apps
    • Build AI and vibe-coded solutions

  5. Build target lists for sales and marketing

    Build highly targeted audiences using hundreds of data points to:

    • Find your best opportunities
    • Improve campaign performance

  6. Analyse your customer base

    Analyse your customers to discover patterns and correlations to:

    • Find more customers
    • Understand what drives buying

  7. Identify at-risk customers and protect revenue

    Overlay customer data with financial data to identify accounts at risk.

    • Spot risk early
    • Take action to retain customers

  8. Monitor changes and get alerts

    Track the schools and trusts that matter and get alerts when important changes happen.

    • React to opportunities
    • Stay informed

  9. Personalise pitches, proposals and campaigns

    Use Education IQ context to inform AI-generated calls, pitches, proposals, emails and campaigns.

    • Create relevant messages
    • Improve win rates

  10. Automate workflows and processes

    Connect Education IQ data and insights to your tools and workflows to automate tasks and decisions.

    • Trigger workflows and tasks
    • Make data-informed decisions

Data is the foundation. Intelligence is the product.

Knowing who exists is useful. Knowing who matters is better. Education IQ exists to close that gap — and where it helps, to put a person alongside the data who can tell you what it means.
