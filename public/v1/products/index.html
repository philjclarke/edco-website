Products

Products built around understanding education

They are part of EdCo, not separate businesses.

EdCo products are different ways of solving education-specific problems. Which of them is relevant depends entirely on what you’re trying to achieve.

  • Education IQ

    The right context, at the point it matters.

    Understand individual schools, trusts and the wider sector — so you can identify need and make every interaction more relevant.

    Read about it

  • Engagement Hub

    Run better events in schools. Build better engagement with educators.

    A website that demonstrates why schools should want to work with you, rather than listing what you sell.

    Read about it

  • SPIRIT

    SPIRIT is an option, not an agenda.

    A fully managed, education-specific CRM — for organisations that want one. If you already have a CRM you like, we’ll work with that instead.

    Read about it

  • International Schools Network

    Part of how organisations reach international educators — and part of how EdCo keeps listening to them.

    ISN serves international-school educators, and gives organisations a credible route to engage with them.

    Read about it

  • The Curriculum Network

    A resource for educators, and a way for organisations to give value and build relationships.

    The Curriculum Network connects EdCo with UK educators through webinars, CPD, research, events and content.

    Read about it

Your technology doesn’t need our logo on it.

If you already have a CRM you like — Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics or anything else — we’ll help make that environment more valuable. SPIRIT is an option, not an agenda.
