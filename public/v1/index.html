The Education Company

Helping organisations build better relationships with education.

Understand educators. Create value. Build engagement.

EdCo helps organisations understand education, engage the right people and build relationships that drive awareness, leads, pipeline and growth.

Tell us what you’re trying to achieve See how EdCo works

The starting point

Schools need partners, not vendors.

Schools aren’t looking for suppliers. They’re looking for solutions to real problems and better outcomes.

If you want a meaningful relationship with educators, understand what they need, be useful, earn their attention and build their trust.

At EdCo we love helping great companies become great partners.

How EdCo works

Listen. Understand. Give. Engage. Build. Grow.

One philosophy underpins everything we do. We start with what you’re trying to achieve, then bring together the right combination of insight, engagement and technology.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Ask educators rather than guess.

    Interviews · Focus groups · Networks · Research · Conversations · Events · Continual sector participation

  2. 02

    Understand

    Use data, intelligence and research to understand where needs exist.

    Education IQ · Customer analysis · Market intelligence · School and trust data · Segmentation

  3. 03

    Give

    Create something educators genuinely value.

    Research · CPD · Events · Guides and articles · Tools and diagnostics · Classroom resources

  4. 04

    Engage

    Put it in front of the right people.

    Digital Marketing · Events · ISN · The Curriculum Network · Opted-in audiences · Targeted communications

  5. 05

    Build

    Turn engagement into relationships, leads and pipeline.

    Education IQ · SPIRIT · Engagement Hub engagement intelligence · Your existing CRM

  6. 06

    Grow

    Strengthen and expand the relationships you’ve already earned.

    Retention risk · Whitespace · Trust opportunities · Cross-sell · Deeper engagement

Why breadth matters

Start with a capability specialist and you often start with their solution. Start with a sector specialist and you start with the problem.

  • A data provider sees a data problem.
  • A marketing agency sees a campaign.
  • A research agency sees a research project.
  • A software company sees something to build.

We start somewhere else.

Sometimes the answer is research. Sometimes it’s better intelligence. Sometimes it’s a different proposition, useful content, an event, a campaign, technology or several of those things together.

And sometimes the best advice is to change the plan altogether.
