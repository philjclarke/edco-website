Education programmes that create genuine value.

All of it, or the part you don’t want to do.

A CSR programme in education only works if it is genuinely useful to schools. That means starting with what educators actually need, not with what makes a good report.

What we can take on

The whole programme, or the pieces that need education expertise.

  • Identifying educational need
  • Programme development
  • Concept development
  • Research
  • Content and resources
  • School identification
  • School recruitment
  • Delivery
  • Technology
  • Events
  • Measurement

Getting into schools is the part people underestimate

Most CSR programmes in education fail at recruitment rather than at design. The idea is sound, the materials are good, and then nobody can get schools to take part.

That is usually where we come in — because identifying the right schools, reaching the right person inside them and giving them a reason to say yes is the same problem we solve for commercial clients, with the same data and the same networks behind it.

Your client stays your client.

EdCo works alongside professional CSR agencies regularly. We are not trying to take ownership of your client relationship, and we will put that in writing if it helps.

Measuring something worth measuring

Reach is the easy number. How many schools, how many pupils, how many downloads. It is also the least interesting thing a programme can report.

We would rather help you measure whether the thing was any use — what changed for the educators who took part, and whether they would do it again.
