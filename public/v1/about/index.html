About EdCo

What we believe

Partnership is demonstrated, not claimed.

We could open with a timeline. It seems more useful to tell you how we think, so you can work out whether you want us anywhere near your plans.

  • 01

    Start with what educators need.

    Your product is fascinating to you. Their problem is fascinating to them.

  • 02

    Listen before you assume.

    Thirty-five years in education has taught us many things. One of them is to keep asking questions.

  • 03

    Give to receive.

    Be useful before you ask someone to buy something.

  • 04

    Partnership is demonstrated, not claimed.

    Putting “trusted education partner” on your homepage doesn’t make you one.

  • 05

    Relevance beats volume.

    Sending the wrong message to more schools doesn’t make it more right.

  • 06

    Data is the foundation. Understanding is the value.

    A database can tell you who exists. Intelligence helps you understand who matters.

  • 07

    Make friends before you make leads.

    People are considerably more receptive to organisations they already value.

  • 08

    Better relationships build better pipeline.

    Yes, we said pipeline. This is ultimately about helping good organisations sell more.

  • 09

    Winning a customer is the beginning, not the end.

    There’s often more opportunity among the organisations that already know you than those that don’t.

  • 10

    Good advice sometimes means saying “don’t do that”.

    If we think a proposed campaign is a bad idea, we’d rather say so before the money is spent.

  • 11

    Your objectives become our objectives.

    We’re more interested in what you’re trying to achieve than which bit of EdCo we can sell you.

  • 12

    Technology should enable the relationship, not become the story.

    Nobody built a better relationship with a school because they had an impressive tech stack.

Dedicated account management

Your objectives become our objectives.

You should have one relationship with EdCo, not separate relationships with several departments.

Your Account Manager’s job isn’t to sell you whichever EdCo service happens to be next. It’s to understand what you’re trying to achieve and bring together the people who can help — then keep the work aligned to it, track progress, and challenge the assumptions when they need challenging.
