What MAT finance leaders are telling us about the next budget round

Trust finance leaders are making decisions earlier, with less certainty, and are far less willing to take a supplier's word for a saving.

28 August 2026

Sample article. Written to the brief’s voice to demonstrate the template. Not approved as EdCo’s published position.

What we’re hearing

Trust finance leaders keep describing the same shape of problem: the decisions are landing earlier in the year than they used to, and with less certainty attached to them.

Several have told us they are now provisionally setting budgets before they have confidence in the numbers underneath them, then revisiting. That changes when a supplier conversation is useful, and it changes what makes it useful.

Where we’re hearing it

Interviews and a small group session run through EdCo’s research programme this term, alongside conversations at trust-facing events.

Why it matters

The window in which a new supplier can influence a decision is moving, and it is moving away from the point at which most suppliers start marketing.

What it could mean for organisations working with education

If your campaign lands when the budget is already provisionally set, you are not competing on merit. You are competing on whether someone is willing to reopen a decision.

Two things follow. The first is timing. The second, and more uncomfortable one, is that a saving claimed in a brochure now carries almost no weight — the finance leaders we spoke to want to see the working.
