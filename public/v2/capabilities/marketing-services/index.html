Start with what you want marketing to achieve.

Sometimes the problem with your marketing isn’t your marketing.

Campaigns, content and channels — organised around your objective rather than our departments.

Channels are tools. They are not the strategy.

Email, content, digital, paid media, creative, targeting and campaign management are all things we do well. None of them is a plan.

If a campaign isn’t working, the campaign often isn’t the problem. The targeting may be fine and the proposition weak. We would rather tell you that before the money is spent than afterwards.

What are you trying to get marketing to do?

Build brand awareness

Become known for something educators care about, not just known.

Generate leads

Reasons to talk, not just addresses to mail.

Build an opted-in audience

An audience you have permission to talk to again.

Recruit to events

Fill the room with the right people, not just the room.

Engage existing customers

The audience most organisations under-market to.

Launch something new

Ideally after we’ve checked somebody wants it.

Campaign around a specific need

One message, one problem, the right organisations.

Re-engage a dormant audience

Where there’s a genuine reason to get back in touch.

Reaching more schools with the wrong message doesn’t make the message any more effective.
