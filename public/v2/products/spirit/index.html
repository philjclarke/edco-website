What SPIRIT offers

  • Lower cost of ownership
  • Fully managed
  • Education data integrated
  • Simple to use
  • Strong support for selling across trusts
  • Dedicated account management

What SPIRIT looks like

And if you already have a CRM?

Then keep it. If your organisation already uses Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics or anything else it gets on with, EdCo will help make that environment more valuable rather than argue with it.

SPIRIT is the right answer when an organisation wants a simpler, fully managed, education-specific CRM. It is not the right answer for everyone, and we won’t pretend otherwise.
